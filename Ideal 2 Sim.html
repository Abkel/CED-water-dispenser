Ideal Design 2 in 3D, simulation only: twin-jar sensing station

The ideal design as first drawn. Two 10 L jars sit on rear drawers, changed from behind; a pump doses 250 mL through a sealed line. A platform in an open bay raises the user's glass to a 25 mm gap under a rigid spout hung from the top slab, and a wet sensor, drip tray and air purge keep the area dry.
