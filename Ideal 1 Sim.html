Ideal Design 1 in 3D, simulation only: passive constant-head station

The ideal design as first drawn, before any FMEA fixes, restyled to follow the cardboard prototype: a narrow cup window on the upper front, an L-shaped jar opening at the bottom that wraps the front-right corner, a top cap and a stocky body. The mechanism is unchanged. A 20 L jar rides on springs so its water surface stays at 1.22 m, a fixed siphon feeds a pinch valve, and the weight of a cup opens the shutter. There are no electrics.
